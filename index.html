Frequently asked questions

Can I enquire about a particular sofa or dining set?

Yes. Send the team a screenshot or product reference through Instagram and ask about availability, size and pricing.

Can I ask about customisation?

Customisation options may vary by product. Contact the showroom team to confirm available fabrics, finishes, colours and sizes.

Can I visit the showroom before deciding?

Yes. Visit the Ballupur Road gallery to experience the furniture in person.

Prices and availability can change. Please request the latest quote directly from the store.
